Implementation plan

What changed after the client meeting, and where each change lives in the prototype. Open the prototype; its “Prototype · change state” panel loads every state listed here.

Read this first

  • Visual baseline. The linked canvas is “Package Selection Redesign”: package, payment, confirmation, Vault and handover screens. It has no funnel, onboarding, Will wizard, Home states or bottom navigation. Those were built new, using the canvas’s colours and components (navy #0d2340, gold #c8a868, parchment, 54px buttons, 16–17px body).
  • Fonts. The canvas uses Cormorant Garamond and DM Sans. The brief names DM Serif Display and Manrope, so the prototype uses those. Switching back is two lines in app.css.
  • Where it lives. Local files in revamp/. Nothing was changed on the shared canvas.
  • Not confirmed by the business. Final prices, the notarized price, when printing unlocks, editing during review, and what happens after notarized payment. Each is one value in config.js; no screen hard-codes it.

B. End-to-end flow

Free Will entry

Campaign → Value journey (problem 1, problem 2, the fix, Vault as optional, free Will) → Create account → Will wizard, chapter 1 → … → Review by chapter → Submit → Scholar Review → Legal Review → Ready to Sign → Sign at home (free) or Notarized (checkout) → Completed → Home, with one Vault discovery banner

Vault entry

Campaign → Vault discovery → Create account → Checkout → Vault dashboard → Home, with “Create your Islamic Will for free” as the one recommendation

Direct entry

Product tour (4 screens, skippable) → Create account → Home, Will not started

  • No plan picker and no “Will or Vault?” question anywhere. The entry decides.
  • Funnel visitors skip the tour: they have just seen the same message.
  • No payment screen before a paid service is chosen.
  • “Save and go Home” is on every wizard screen. Answers save on every keystroke, and Continue on Home returns to the same section.

Priority order, as built

  1. Required action (a reviewer’s question): takes the main card, no banner.
  2. Continue the Will: no banner.
  3. Review status or signing in the main card. Signing shows no banner.
  4. Missing critical information: “Add your assets.”
  5. Family access: “Keep your family in the loop.”
  6. Vault discovery, only if the Vault is not owned and the Will is submitted or complete.
  7. A relevant add-on: for Vault-only users, the free Will.
  8. Maintenance: “Review your plan.”

I. Progress and status logic

  • Two levels, never mixed. The line “Chapter 2 of 6 · Family” is chapter progress. The segments under it are sections of that chapter and are labelled “Section 1 of 3”. A chapter with one section shows no segments.
  • Chapters are data. Order and sections come from chapters in config. The Guardian section appears only when a child under 18 was added.
  • Stages are data. Submitted → Scholar Review → Legal Review → Ready to Sign → Attestation (Notarized route only) → Completed.
  • Each review stage answers three things: what is happening now, whether anything is needed from the user, what comes next.
