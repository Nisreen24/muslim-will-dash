Remaining blockers

Critical — must be resolved before a full handoff

  1. The generated Will document and Appendix A / B. “View my Will” shows answers by chapter. What the printed document looks like, and what the appendices contain, is not designed. Needs a reference.
  2. Inheritance shares must come from the real table. The journey and the review show shares from a stand-in calculator. Development must replace it with the API’s table, and a scholar must confirm what is shown before any visitor sees a number.

Medium — should be fixed or decided

  1. Phone versions of the ten Vault pages and the attestation pages. Built for desktop only. Decide whether the phone deliverable is the approved prototype or this phone build; if this build, the ten pages need adding.
  2. Alternate executor. Shown on the client’s Vault pages, not collected in the Will.
  3. What a witness sees. Witnesses are shown as invited, but the invitation and their confirmation step are not designed.
  4. When Document Upload opens. Here it opens after choosing and paying for the notarized route. The live site shows it earlier. Needs a decision.
  5. Password rules differ between desktop and phone. Five rules vs 8 characters.
  6. Confirmations listed in the checklist. Prices, Fully Fledged, Family Plan, section names, stage name, print timing, review dates, logo, legal texts.
  7. Tablet widths. Built, not reviewed screen by screen.

Minor — polish

  1. 4K not measured. Expected to match 2560.
  2. Totals add CAD only. Other currencies are listed, not converted.
  3. Profile route is reachable only from the avatar menu. By design; noted because the link crawl does not follow hidden menus.

Fixed during this audit

  • Obligations: optional proof upload added to the form.
  • Witnesses: invitation status with Resend now shows after submission.
  • Estate Inventory: the category dropdown became filter chips.
  • Document Upload and attestation scheduling built from the live-site screenshots supplied afterwards; the review stage is now named Conformity Review.

How it was checked

  • Flow walk-throughs. Desktop: 85 assertions from the first journey step to a closed and reopened Vault, at 1440, then shell checks at 1280 and 1024. Phone: 40 assertions. Both pass.
  • Link and action crawl. From each user state (18 on desktop, 15 on phone) every in-app link was followed: 290 screen visits on desktop, 149 on phone. No dead link, no empty screen, no button without an action, no script error. The crawl does not open hidden menus or submit forms; those paths are covered by the walk-throughs.
  • Width check. 26 major screens at 1024, 1280, 1366, 1440, 1920 and 2560: no sideways scrolling, content never wider than 1480, forms never wider than 760, sidebar 224–280, no text under 15px, no control under 44px, no top-bar collisions.
  • Accessibility audit. 48 desktop and 19 phone screens: contrast, text size, target size, one primary button, field labels, control names, visible keyboard focus. No findings.
  • Read, not measured. Copy length, tone, and whether whitespace feels balanced were judged from screenshots of a sample of screens, not all of them.
  • Not checked. 3840px, tablet widths screen by screen, real browsers other than Chrome, screen readers, printing.
